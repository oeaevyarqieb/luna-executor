## Installation

> Windows only -- Android and iOS support is on the roadmap.

1. Go to **[Releases](https://github.com/oeaevyarqieb/luna-executor/releases/latest)** and download `Setup.exe`
2. Right-click `Setup.exe` and select **Run as Administrator**
3. Open Roblox and join any game
4. In Luna, click **Inject** and wait for the green **Attached** confirmation
5. Paste or load your script and click **Execute**

> **Tip:** Always wait for your character to fully load into the game world before injecting.

<summary><strong>What if Luna fails to launch after a Windows update?</strong></summary>
